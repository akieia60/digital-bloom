## 🌟 **SORA (BEST QUALITY!)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**What It Is:** OpenAI's professional video generator (part of ChatGPT Plus/Pro)

**Access:** chatgpt.com (if you have ChatGPT Plus or Pro)

### **How to Use Sora:**

**1. Go to ChatGPT**
```
https://chatgpt.com
```

**2. Click on Sora (if available with your plan)**

**3. Use These Optimized Prompts:**

**For Red Roses:**
```
A premium bouquet of deep red roses with fresh green foliage,
slowly rotating on white marble. Cinematic lighting from soft window light,
shallow depth of field, professional florist photography, romantic elegant atmosphere,
ultra-realistic detail, 1080p
```

**For Spring Flowers:**
```
Vibrant spring flower arrangement with pink tulips and white daisies,
gentle rotation, natural sunlight, fresh garden aesthetic,
professional quality, soft focus background, cheerful and bright,
high definition
```

**4. Generate Video**
- Sora creates 5-20 second videos
- Download as MP4
- Save to: Desktop/flower-shop/public/videos/

### **Sora Pro Tips:**
- ✅ Best quality of all AI video tools
- ✅ Most realistic
- ✅ Great for professional product videos
- ✅ No watermark
- ⚠️ Requires ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) or Pro ($200/mo)

## 🎨 **OpenArt** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**What It Is:** AI image and video generator

**Website:** https://openart.ai

### **How to Use OpenArt for Flower Videos:**

**1. Sign in to OpenArt**

**2. Choose "Video" Mode**

**3. Use Adapted Prompts:**

**Elegant Rose Arrangement:**
```
Luxurious red rose bouquet, slow 360 rotation, professional florist studio,
soft dramatic lighting, white background, elegant presentation,
4K quality, detailed petals
```

**Mixed Spring Bouquet:**
```
Colorful spring flowers in glass vase, gentle rotating movement,
natural daylight, fresh and vibrant, professional photography,
detailed textures, HD quality
```

**4. Settings:**
- Motion: 3-4 (moderate movement)
- Duration: 5-10 seconds
- Quality: High/Max

**5. Download & Save:**
```
Desktop/flower-shop/public/videos/
```

### **OpenArt Pro Tips:**
- ✅ Good for creating flower images first, then animating
- ✅ Free credits available
- ✅ Can upscale quality
- 💡 Create still image first, then add motion

## 🚀 **Higgsfield (HiggsFI)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**What It Is:** Social AI video creation platform

**Access:** Mobile app or web at higgsfield.ai

### **How to Use Higgsfield:**

**1. Open Higgsfield App/Website**

**2. Select "Create Video"**

**3. Use Short, Focused Prompts:**

**For Higgsfield, Keep It Simple:**
```
Red roses rotating slowly, professional florist, soft lighting
```

```
Spring tulips in vase, gentle movement, natural light
```

```
White lilies elegant arrangement, slow rotation, clean background
```

**4. Settings:**
- Style: Realistic/Photographic
- Length: 5-10 seconds
- Motion: Smooth

**5. Export as MP4**

### **Higgsfield Pro Tips:**
- ✅ Mobile-friendly
- ✅ Quick generation
- ✅ Good for social media style
- 💡 Works best with shorter, simpler prompts

## 💡 **Pro Tips for YOUR Tools**

### **Sora:**
- Use VERY detailed prompts
- Mention camera movement
- Specify lighting clearly
- Add mood/atmosphere
- Request 4K/cinematic quality

### **OpenArt:**
- Create image first, then animate
- Use "Motion" slider carefully
- Start with 3-5 second clips
- Can upscale after generation

### **Higgsfield:**
- Keep prompts SHORT
- Focus on main subject
- Simple movements work best
- Great for quick iterations

### **ChatGPT:**
- Perfect for static product photos
- Can refine with follow-up prompts
- "Make it more elegant/romantic/bright"
- Download and animate in OpenArt
