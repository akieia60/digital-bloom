DIGITAL BLOOM - HOW TO ADD NEW VIDEOS & PHOTOS
================================================================================

FOLDER STRUCTURE:
----------------
digital bloom/
  ├── digital-flower-shop_1.html  (Your website file)
  ├── media/
  │   ├── videos/  (Put all MP4 videos here)
  │   └── images/  (Put all PNG/JPG/GIF images here)
  └── HOW-TO-ADD-VIDEOS.txt  (This file)


STEP-BY-STEP: ADD A NEW VIDEO
================================================================================

STEP 1: Save Your Video
------------------------
1. Create or download your new flower video
2. Save it to: digital bloom/media/videos/
   Example: my-awesome-flower.mp4


STEP 2: Copy the Template Code
-------------------------------
1. Open digital-flower-shop_1.html in a text editor (Notepad, TextEdit, VS Code, etc.)
2. Find the "Easy Upload Template" card (has dashed border)
3. Copy this ENTIRE block of code (the template below):

<!-- COPY THIS TEMPLATE ↓ -->
                <div class="flower-card">
                    <div class="badge" style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #FFD700, #FFA500);">Premium 3D</div>
                    <div class="flower-img" style="height: 450px; background: linear-gradient(180deg, #f5f5f5, #ffffff); position: relative; overflow: hidden;">
                        <video
                            autoplay
                            loop
                            muted
                            playsinline
                            style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover;">
                            <source src="media/videos/YOUR-VIDEO-FILENAME.mp4" type="video/mp4">
                            Your browser doesn't support video.
                        </video>
                    </div>
                    <div class="flower-info">
                        <h3 class="flower-name">Your Flower Name</h3>
                        <p class="flower-desc">Your description here - what makes this video special?</p>
                        <div class="flower-price">$12.99</div>
                        <button class="buy-btn" onclick="purchase('Your Flower Name', '$12.99')">Get This Video 🎬</button>
                    </div>
                </div>
<!-- COPY THIS TEMPLATE ↑ -->


STEP 3: Paste BEFORE the Template Card
---------------------------------------
1. Paste the copied code BEFORE the "Easy Upload Template" card
   (The template card should always stay at the end)


STEP 4: Update Your Video Details
----------------------------------
Change these 4 things:

1. VIDEO FILENAME:
   src="media/videos/YOUR-VIDEO-FILENAME.mp4"
   ↓
   src="media/videos/my-awesome-flower.mp4"

2. FLOWER NAME (appears twice - keep them the same):
   <h3 class="flower-name">Your Flower Name</h3>
   ↓
   <h3 class="flower-name">Sunset Dreams</h3>

   AND in the button:
   onclick="purchase('Your Flower Name', '$12.99')"
   ↓
   onclick="purchase('Sunset Dreams', '$12.99')"

3. DESCRIPTION:
   <p class="flower-desc">Your description here...</p>
   ↓
   <p class="flower-desc">Beautiful 3D sunset roses perfect for romantic TikToks.</p>

4. PRICE (optional - default is $12.99):
   <div class="flower-price">$12.99</div>
   ↓
   <div class="flower-price">$15.99</div>


STEP 5: Save & View
-------------------
1. Save the HTML file
2. Open digital-flower-shop_1.html in your browser
3. Your new video should appear!

TIPS & TRICKS:
================================================================================

✅ BADGE OPTIONS:
   Change the badge text and color:
   - Premium 3D: style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #FFD700, #FFA500);"
   - AI Art: style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #FF1744, #FF80AB);"
   - New: style="background: #4CAF50;"
   - Limited: style="background: #FF6B6B;"
   - Bestseller: (default red - no style needed)

✅ PRICING:
   Images: $9.99
   Standard 3D videos: $12.99
   Premium/longer videos: $14.99-$19.99

✅ BUTTON TEXT:
   For videos: "Get This Video 🎬"
   For images: "Get This Image 🖼️"

✅ VIDEO SIZING:
   - object-fit: cover (fills the space, may crop)
   - object-fit: contain (shows full video, may have black bars)

✅ GRID LAYOUT:
   To make a card take 2 columns (wider):
   <div class="flower-card" style="grid-column: span 2;">


TROUBLESHOOTING:
================================================================================

Q: Video doesn't play?
A: Check the filename is correct and the video is in media/videos/ folder

Q: Video path shows wrong?
A: Make sure path is: media/videos/filename.mp4 (no slash at start)

Q: Want to reorder videos?
A: Cut and paste the entire card block to move it

Q: How to delete a video from the site?
A: Delete the entire <div class="flower-card">...</div> block

Q: Videos slowing down the page?
A: Remove autoplay from some videos or use poster images
