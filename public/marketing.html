What the data says

  • Instagram is the workhorse. $2.70 CPM is excellent for a luxury-leaning brand. Steady spend belongs here.
  • TikTok varies wildly. Same $760 quote shows a 5K floor → 50K ceiling. Worth a small test ($240 to match IG) before scaling.
  • Facebook 30-day = waste. But the same $150 spent in 1 day reaches ~19× more people. The Facebook algo punishes long, low-daily-spend campaigns and rewards concentrated bursts. So Facebook becomes a burst tactic on key dates, not an always-on channel.

Tier 2 — Scale the Winner ($5,000–$10,000 through Father's Day)

What the budget looks like if Tier 1 produces clear conversion data and we want to lean into the channel that worked.

  • If IG is the winner: $4K Instagram steady spend + $500 TikTok lifestyle creator partnerships + $1,500 Facebook bursts on every holiday eve through summer. ~600K – 1M reach.
  • If TikTok unexpectedly hits: $3K TikTok + $2K Instagram + $1K creator collabs. Higher reach ceiling but less control.
  • Conservative path: $5K total — $2,500 IG, $1K TikTok test extension, $1,500 Facebook bursts. ~300K – 500K reach.

Decision point: 7 days after Mother's Day. If conversion came in below 1%, we hold at Tier 1 and rework creative. Above 2%, we ramp.

Tier 3 — Global Blitz ($50,000+ across 17 markets)

The dream number. Not asking for it yet — flagging that it exists.

Digital Bloom is translated into 10 languages and the brand is positioned for 17 international markets (Japan, Germany, France, Mexico, Brazil, Cuba, Dominican Republic, Jamaica, multiple African nations, Haiti, Philippines, Netherlands, England, China, Canada, El Salvador, plus US). A real global push to make the brand known across all of them simultaneously requires $140K – $350K in paid spend at industry-average CPMs. That's a Series-seed conversation, not a friend-and-family ask.

The realistic ramp is Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3 over 12–18 months, funded by a combination of revenue from the $1 Mother's Day promo, founder investment, and aligned supporters who believe in the vision early.

The honest stakeholder summary

Digital Bloom is a brand-new category — luxury digital gifting in a world where physical flowers are the default. The category has to be created before it can be scaled.

Right now I need $1,000 for the next 30 days to run the Tier 1 test, prove the conversion math, and earn the right to ask for Tier 2. Anything beyond that goes directly into Instagram steady spend (since that's the cheapest, highest-yield channel) or into Father's Day burst budget if Mother's Day proves the model.

If a supporter wants to help fund the gap between Tier 1 and Tier 2, that's the cleanest leverage point — it lets us scale the winning platform without cutting through holiday momentum.

What I won't do with this money

  • Pour all of it into one platform without a test. The 30-day Facebook quote at $100 CPM proves how easy it is to waste money if you don't read the algo's rhythm.
  • Boost organic posts at random times. Every paid dollar is timed to a specific holiday date or a specific platform's known sweet spot.
  • Pay for follower counts. The metric I track is reach × intent — meaning did someone who needs a Mother's Day gift see this.
