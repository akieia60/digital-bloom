Gamble's vision — verbatim, then translated

"Take every digital flyer we created to market Digital Bloom and put it in Buffer to be posted on each social media platform — Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Pinterest. If we have 10 or 15 different digital flyers they all need to go up on each account. In a 3-day span every one of those flyers need to be pushed out as wide as possible in all of our target markets — America, Japan, Germany, France, Mexico, Brazil, Cuba, Dominican Republic, Jamaica, Africa, Haiti, Philippines, Netherlands, England, China, Canada, El Salvador. Reach 1, 2, 4 or 5 million people per market. Total 20 to 50 million people. There are 1 billion people on social media. We need to reach everyone we can in a 3-day time span in order for our campaign to go viral."

Translated to operations

  • Volume: 22 flyers × 5 platforms × 17 markets = ~1,870 unique post slots over 72 hours.
  • Cadence: ~26 posts per platform-market over 3 days — every 2.7 hours per channel. Heavy but Buffer can queue it.
  • Reach goal: 20–50M cumulative impressions; 6–18M unique reach (impressions ÷ 2.5–3 typical).
  • Budget reality: 20–50M IMPRESSIONS at average $7 CPM = $140K–$350K paid. Organic alone won't get there in 72 hours from a cold-start account.

What 20M impressions actually costs. Blended global average ≈ $7 CPM weighted by population × buying power. 20,000,000 ÷ 1,000 × 7 = $140,000. The "reach 20M" goal is ~$140K of paid spend. The "reach 50M" goal is ~$350K. Those are real numbers — and impressions ≠ unique people. Unique reach is typically 35–55% of impressions, so $140K of impressions ≈ 7–11M unique people.
What gets you "viral" in 3 days for $5K. A focused $5K paid seed (heavy on top 3 markets — US, Brazil, Mexico — with lookalike audiences and 5–7% CTR creative) buys ~700K–1.2M impressions, reaches ~300K–500K unique people, and gives the algorithm enough signal to maybe compound organically into 5–20M reach if even 0.3% of viewers reshare. Viral compounding is the only way to hit 20M+ on a small business budget — paid alone won't do it.

Budget tiers — five honest scenarios

  • What it buys: All 22 flyers queued through Buffer to 5 platforms × 17 markets, plus auto-follow tools (TokFollowers $49, Inflact $59, Combin $25) for the seed month.
  • Realistic reach: 20K–80K unique people, depending on existing follower count and algorithm luck.
  • Time to ship: 4 hours of Gam queueing + 72 hours of Buffer auto-posting.
  • When to use: if Stripe revenue is < $500/wk and we can't justify paid spend yet.

Tier 1 — Lean Paid · $2,800–$5,000

  • What it buys: Tier 0 + targeted boost on top 5 markets (US, Brazil, Mexico, Philippines, UK). $1,200 Meta + $800 TikTok + $400 YouTube + $200 X + tools/translation.
  • Realistic reach: 500K–1.2M unique people; 1.5M–3M impressions.
  • Time to ship: 4–6 hours of Gam queueing + 24h ad-set creation.
  • When to use: Mother's Day push (this week) or any single-event campaign. This is the recommended starting tier.

Tier 2 — Aggressive Paid · $8,500–$15,000

  • What it buys: Tier 1 + Germany + France + Japan + Pinterest broad. $3,500 Meta + $2,500 TikTok + $1,200 YouTube + $400 X + $400 Douyin (China overseas) + $250 Pinterest + tools.
  • Realistic reach: 1.5M–3M unique people; 4M–8M impressions.
  • Time to ship: 1 day setup + 72h push.
  • When to use: after Tier 1 hits CTR > 2% and CPM < $8 — scale up the winners.

Tier 3 — Viral Chase · $30,000–$60,000

  • What it buys: Tier 2 + paid creator partnerships ($10K), broad-reach campaigns in all 17 markets, A/B-test 4 hooks per flyer, Douyin paid via partner agency for China-mainland sliver.
  • Realistic reach: 4M–8M unique people; 10M–18M impressions.
  • Time to ship: 1 week prep + 72h push.
  • When to use: after $50K+ in revenue OR with outside funding (Amber Grant, Hello Alice).
  • Funded by: the cumulative grant program + early Stripe revenue.

Tier 4 — Gamble's Dream · $140,000–$400,000

  • What it buys: Gamble's literal vision — 20M to 50M impressions across 17 markets in 3 days, with paid spend across all 5 platforms at the buying-power-weighted CPMs from the table above.
  • Realistic reach: 7M–18M unique people (50% of impressions).
  • Time to ship: 2-3 weeks prep + 72h push.
  • When to use: Series-seed funding, BlueVine line of credit at $250K, or after $500K in revenue.

Brianna's reach research — what she actually tested

Brianna (marketing specialist, hired 2026-05-06) ran reach quotes through each platform's native ad manager with our content. Hand-written notes 2026-05-07. CPM = cost per 1,000 reached.

The headline: Instagram is by far the cheapest reach. TikTok is mid-tier. Facebook is terrible spread over 30 days but actually decent if you spend the same money in 1 day — the algo rewards concentrated bursts.
Platform Spend Window Reach CPM Verdict
Instagram $150 30 days 55,851 $2.69 Cheapest
Instagram $240 30 days 47K – 88,417 $2.71 – $5.10 Still good
TikTok $760 30 days 5K – 50,000 $15 – $152 Volatile · 20 % off promos sometimes
Facebook $150 30 days 1,500 $100 Don't do this
Facebook $250 30 days 2,200 $114 Worse
Facebook $150 1 day ~28,000 $5.36 Burst works

What this tells us

  • IG is the workhorse. $2.70 CPM is excellent for a luxury vertical. Pour the steady spend here.
  • TikTok varies wildly. Same $760 quote shows 5K floor → 50K ceiling. Worth a small test ($240 to match IG) before committing — only worth scaling if it lands closer to the 50K end.
  • FB 30-day = waste. The algo deprioritizes long, low-daily-spend campaigns. Same $150 spent in 1 day reaches ~19× more people. So FB is a burst tactic, not an always-on channel — boost a flyer the day before Mother's Day, then go quiet.

Buffer mechanics — what fits, what doesn't

Buffer is connected as of 2026-05-02 with the 6-channel plan ($35.99/mo via Apple IAP). What that actually buys:

  • Channels: 6 connected — IG, TikTok, Pinterest, FB, YouTube, + 1 spare. X is NOT in the 6. Adding X = upgrade to higher Buffer tier or post X manually.
  • Queue size: Unlimited posts per channel on the paid plan.
  • Geo-targeting: Buffer does NOT do per-post geo-targeting on most platforms — it just publishes. Geo-targeting happens INSIDE the platform's ad manager (Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads, etc.) for paid promotion.
  • Native multi-market: Each platform account = one market. To run "US Instagram" + "Brazil Instagram" + "Mexico Instagram" you need 3 separate IG accounts, each connected to Buffer as a separate channel. Our 6 channels = roughly 1 market.
Buffer scaling reality. Gamble's vision of "every platform × every market" through Buffer hits a hard cap at 6 channels. To do all 5 platforms × 17 markets = 85 channels would need a $300+/mo Buffer plan or a competitor like Hootsuite Enterprise. The realistic move: post English creative globally on the 6 channels we have, and let Meta/TikTok ad-manager geo-target paid distribution to specific markets. Localized creative (translated voiceovers, language-specific copy) goes through paid ads, not Buffer organic.
