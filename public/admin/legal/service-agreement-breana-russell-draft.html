and

Company and Contractor are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1. Engagement & Role

Company hereby engages Contractor to act as Digital Bloom's Lead Social Media Manager & Influencer Partner, and Contractor accepts such engagement, on the terms set forth below. Contractor shall perform the Services as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner, or agent of Company.

2. Services

Contractor shall perform the following services (collectively, the "Services"):

2.1 Account Configuration & Setup

  1. Audit, optimize, and properly configure all existing Digital Bloom social-media accounts, including without limitation: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X (Twitter), Pinterest, YouTube, Threads, and any future platforms designated by Company;
  2. Configure profile bios, link-in-bio destinations, branding (avatar, banner, highlights, story templates), and analytics integrations consistent with Digital Bloom brand standards;
  3. Connect each account to Company's Buffer scheduling tool (or successor) so that all posting can be queued, monitored, and audited centrally.

2.2 Daily Posting & Content Distribution

  1. Maintain a daily posting cadence across designated platforms, subject to platform-specific best practices (e.g., 1–3 IG posts/stories/day, 1–4 TikTok/day, 5–10 Pinterest pins/day, etc.);
  2. Distribute Company-supplied digital flyers, blooms, and brand assets according to a posting calendar approved by Company;
  3. Caption, hashtag, and tag posts in a manner consistent with Digital Bloom brand voice;
  4. Engage with audience comments, DMs, and mentions in a timely and on-brand manner.

2.3 Influencer & Reach Push

  1. Leverage Contractor's personal influencer presence to amplify Digital Bloom content where appropriate and where Contractor's audience overlaps Digital Bloom's target market;
  2. Coordinate with the global-push campaign plan published by Company at digitalbloom.store/admin/campaigns/global-push.html, or any successor campaign;
  3. Recommend collaborators, sponsorships, and creator partnerships to Company; execute approved partnerships.

2.4 Reporting

  1. Deliver to Company a written weekly performance report covering reach, impressions, follower growth, engagement rate, top-performing content, click-through to digitalbloom.store, and notable audience-feedback themes;
  2. Make all account analytics accessible to Company at any time on request.

4. Term & Termination

  1. Initial Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of six (6) months, unless terminated earlier as provided below.
  2. Renewal. After the initial term, this Agreement renews automatically on a month-to-month basis unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least fifteen (15) days before the end of the then-current term.
  3. Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by giving the other Party at least thirty (30) days' written notice.
  4. Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement or the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) days of written notice describing the breach. Material breaches by Contractor include, without limitation, any breach of Section 7 (Account Ownership) or any breach of the brand-privacy covenants in the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement.
  5. Effect of Termination. On termination, Contractor shall: (i) immediately cease all activity on Company-owned social-media accounts, (ii) surrender all account credentials, recovery emails/phones, two-factor backup codes, and admin roles to a Company representative within forty-eight (48) hours, (iii) deliver to Company any unfinished content, analytics dashboards, follower lists, and brand assets in Contractor's possession, and (iv) be paid for Services properly rendered through the termination date.

5. Independent Contractor Status

Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of Company. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, franchise, or employment relationship. Contractor controls the means and methods of performing the Services subject to the deliverable standards in this Agreement. Contractor is not eligible for any employee benefits, paid time off, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance from Company.

6. Work Product & Intellectual Property

All content, captions, hashtag strategies, posting calendars, follower lists, analytics reports, derivative works, and any other deliverables created by Contractor in connection with the Services (collectively, "Work Product") are "works made for hire" under the U.S. Copyright Act. To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as a work made for hire, Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title, and interest worldwide in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual-property rights. Contractor agrees to execute any documents reasonably requested by Company to perfect such assignment.

Contractor retains a non-exclusive, royalty-free right to display the Work Product in Contractor's personal portfolio, provided that such display does not breach the brand-privacy covenants in the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement.

7. Account Ownership (Material Term)

All social-media accounts, advertising accounts, ad credit balances, follower bases, email aliases, vendor accounts, and digital properties used in connection with the Services are and shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Creative Vision LLC. Contractor shall not:

  1. Change recovery email addresses, recovery phone numbers, or two-factor settings on any Company account without Company's prior written consent;
  2. Move, redirect, or rename any Company account or its handle;
  3. Claim ownership, intellectual-property rights, or transfer rights in any Company account;
  4. Migrate followers, audience lists, or content to any account not owned by Company.

Breach of this Section 7 constitutes a material breach of this Agreement and entitles Company to injunctive relief, all account-related damages (including reasonable estimated value of lost followers and ad credits), and reasonable attorneys' fees.

8. Brand-Voice Approval & Posting Authority

  1. Contractor shall post on Company accounts only content that is on-brand, on-message, and consistent with the standards published by Company (including the standing rules in the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement and any internal style guide Company shares).
  2. For any post that introduces a new product category, makes a pricing claim, references Company financials, or could reasonably be construed as legal, medical, financial, or political advice, Contractor shall obtain Company's prior written approval (email, iMessage, or Buffer-comment approval is sufficient).
  3. Contractor shall not respond to legal threats, press inquiries, customer-service refund disputes above $50, or government/regulator contacts; such matters must be escalated to Company within twenty-four (24) hours.

9. Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months after its termination, Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly: (a) solicit any current customer of Digital Bloom for any competing flower-video, digital-card, or cinematic-greeting product or service; (b) solicit any current contractor or vendor of Digital Bloom to terminate their relationship with Company; or (c) use Company's Confidential Information to identify, target, or solicit followers acquired during the engagement for any competing service.

(Note: This Agreement does not contain a general non-compete clause. Contractor remains free to operate her personal influencer brand and to manage social media for non-competing brands during and after the term, subject to the confidentiality and brand-privacy obligations.)

10. Representations & Warranties

Each Party represents and warrants that: (a) it has full authority to enter into this Agreement; (b) entering into and performing this Agreement does not breach any obligation it has to a third party; (c) the Services and Work Product, as delivered, will not knowingly infringe any third-party copyright, trademark, right of publicity, or other intellectual-property right; and (d) it will comply with all applicable laws, including FTC endorsement and disclosure rules (e.g., #ad / #sponsored on paid posts).

11. Indemnification

Each Party (the "Indemnifying Party") shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other Party from and against any claims, losses, damages, liabilities, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising out of or relating to: (a) the Indemnifying Party's breach of this Agreement; (b) the Indemnifying Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of law; or (c) any claim that the Indemnifying Party's contributions infringe a third-party intellectual-property right.

12. Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of confidentiality, breaches of Section 7 (Account Ownership), or indemnification obligations, neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages, regardless of the form of action. Each Party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total compensation paid or payable to Contractor under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

13. Confidentiality

The Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement signed contemporaneously by the Parties is incorporated by reference into this Agreement and survives termination of this Agreement in accordance with its terms. In the event of conflict between the two documents, the document providing greater protection to Confidential Information controls.

14. Governing Law & Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina, and each Party consents to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.

15. Miscellaneous

  1. Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement of the Parties and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous communications.
  2. Amendment. No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.
  3. Assignment. Contractor may not assign this Agreement without Company's prior written consent. Company may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.
  4. Severability. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.
  5. Counterparts & Electronic Signatures. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.
  6. Notices. Notices under this Agreement may be delivered by email to the email address listed for each Party at the top of this Agreement.
