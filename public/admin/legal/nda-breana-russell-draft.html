MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Effective Date: [Date contract is signed]

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between:

Creative Vision LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company doing business as Digital Bloom, with a principal place of business at 1914 J N Pease Pl, Suite 92343, Charlotte, NC 28262 ("Company"),
and
BreAna Russell, an individual residing at [BreAna's address — street, city, state, zip], contact email [BreAna's email], contact phone [BreAna's phone] ("Recipient").

Company and Recipient are each a "Party" and together the "Parties." Each Party may, in the course of the engagement contemplated by the Parties, share Confidential Information with the other.

1. Purpose

The Parties are entering into this Agreement so that they may share Confidential Information for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating, and performing a social-media management and influencer-marketing engagement under which Recipient will configure, operate, and grow the social-media presence of Digital Bloom (the "Purpose").

2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information, in any form, disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") that is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

  1. The full legal names, home addresses, dates of birth, and personal contact information of Company's owners and personnel (specifically including Akieia M. Davis and Derric M. Williams), which Company maintains as faceless-brand information;
  2. Business plans, marketing strategies, product roadmaps, internal financials, pricing models, and customer lists of Digital Bloom;
  3. Login credentials, API keys, OAuth tokens, account recovery codes, and access to Digital Bloom social-media accounts, advertising accounts, payment processors (including Stripe), banking platforms (including Mercury), and any other vendor or platform Company uses;
  4. Brand assets, prompt formulas, video-generation pipelines, voiceover scripts, character references, and any other creative output produced by or on behalf of Company;
  5. Vendor and contractor relationships, including identities of and terms with any service provider Company engages;
  6. The contents of any unreleased marketing campaign, including the document at digitalbloom.store/admin/campaigns/global-push.html and any successor planning documents;
  7. Any information clearly marked "Confidential" or "Proprietary" or shared with a reasonable expectation of confidentiality.

3. Exclusions from Confidential Information

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can prove: (a) was rightfully known to it without obligation of confidentiality before disclosure by the Disclosing Party; (b) is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party; (c) is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party who is not under an obligation of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

  1. Use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and not for any other use, including any personal or competing business use;
  2. Hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence and protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own most sensitive information, but in no event less than reasonable care;
  3. Not disclose, publish, or distribute the Confidential Information to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent;
  4. Limit access to Confidential Information to those personnel, advisors, or contractors of the Receiving Party who have a clear need to know for the Purpose, and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement;
  5. Promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any actual or suspected unauthorized use, disclosure, or loss of Confidential Information.

5. Specific Brand-Privacy Covenants (Material Term)

Recipient acknowledges that Digital Bloom operates as a faceless brand and agrees that, both during and after the term of this Agreement, Recipient shall not:

  1. Publicly associate the names Akieia Davis, Akieia M. Davis, Derric Williams, or Derric M. Williams with Digital Bloom, Creative Vision LLC, the website digitalbloom.store, or any social-media account operated for the Company;
  2. Disclose any home address, personal email, personal phone number, or date of birth belonging to any Company owner or personnel;
  3. Reference Company personnel by their personal social-media accounts, family members, or geographic location in any public-facing post, comment, story, reel, video, or paid advertisement;
  4. Tag, mention, or link to any personal account of any Company owner or personnel from any Company-operated social-media account.

Breach of this Section 5 constitutes a material breach of this Agreement and entitles Company to immediate injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

6. Account Ownership

All social-media accounts, advertising accounts, vendor accounts, email aliases, and digital properties created, accessed, or operated by Recipient on behalf of Digital Bloom are and shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Creative Vision LLC. Recipient shall not change recovery email addresses, recovery phone numbers, two-factor settings, or ownership claims on any such account without Company's express prior written consent. On termination of the engagement for any reason, Recipient shall immediately surrender all credentials, access tokens, and recovery information to a Company representative and shall not retain any copy.

7. No License Granted

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to grant any rights to either Party under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual-property right of the other Party. All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party.

8. Return or Destruction

Upon written request from the Disclosing Party, or upon termination of the engagement contemplated by the Purpose for any reason, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control, including all copies, notes, summaries, derivatives, and electronic files, and shall certify in writing the completion of such return or destruction.

9. Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for five (5) years from the Effective Date. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the obligations of confidentiality with respect to information that constitutes a trade secret shall survive for so long as the information continues to qualify as a trade secret under applicable law.

10. Equitable Relief

The Parties acknowledge that money damages alone may not provide adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement and that the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek temporary, preliminary, and permanent injunctive relief, specific performance, and any other equitable remedies, in addition to any other relief available at law, without the necessity of posting a bond.

11. Governing Law & Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina, and each Party consents to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.

12. Miscellaneous

  1. Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with any independent-contractor or service agreement entered into between the Parties, constitutes the entire agreement of the Parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous communications.
  2. Amendment. No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.
  3. Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
  4. Counterparts & Electronic Signatures. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
  5. No Employment Relationship. This Agreement does not create any employment, partnership, joint-venture, or agency relationship between the Parties.
